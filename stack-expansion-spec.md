# Stack Expansion Spec

**Status: COMPLETE — historical.** The 10-service expansion documented here was fully
planned (2026-08-28) and deployed (2026-08-29). The spec's content — interview
decisions, phase plans, port allocations, draft compose blocks, image verification
findings, and the nzbdav category rollout runbook — has been consolidated into
[HISTORY.md](HISTORY.md#the-2026-08-28-expansion).

**Outcome:** 8 of 10 planned services are live (Lidarr, Readarr, Bazarr, Audiobookshelf,
Komga, AdGuard Home, CrowdSec, Vaultwarden). Uptime Kuma was removed from scope and
n8n was removed by decision shortly after deploy — see
[HISTORY.md — Retired services](HISTORY.md#retired-services).

For the current state of the stack, see [README.md](README.md) and
[AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).
